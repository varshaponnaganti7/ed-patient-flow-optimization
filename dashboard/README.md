#  Emergency Department Dashboard

##  Overview

This dashboard provides an interactive visualization of **high-acuity patient flow** in an emergency department.

It complements the machine learning model by offering **descriptive insights** into patient patterns, helping stakeholders understand trends and support operational decisions.

---

##  Live Dashboard

##  Key Visualizations

###  High Acuity by Hour

* Identifies peak time periods for critical patient arrivals
* Helps optimize staffing and resource allocation

###  High Acuity by Arrival Mode

* Highlights the role of ambulance vs walk-in patients
* Shows that ambulance arrivals contribute significantly to high-acuity cases

###  High Acuity by Department

* Compares workload across departments
* Helps identify departments handling the most critical cases

###  High Acuity by Gender

* Provides demographic distribution of high-acuity patients
* Useful for understanding population-level patterns

---

##  Insights & Impact

* High-acuity cases are concentrated in specific time windows
* Ambulance arrivals are strongly associated with critical patients
* Certain departments handle disproportionately high critical workloads
* Patient demographics show relatively balanced distribution

This dashboard works alongside the machine learning pipeline:

*  Dashboard → Explains *what is happening*
*  Model → Predicts *what will happen*

Together, they provide a **complete decision-support system**.
